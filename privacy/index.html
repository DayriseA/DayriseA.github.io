In short

  • The site sets no cookie and uses no analytics.
  • If you use the AI assistant, your questions are kept for 30 days, with no account and no IP address in clear, so that I can improve it.
  • A job offer you have analyzed is never stored.
  • You can write to me to access your data or ask for its deletion: adrien.maisak@gmail.com.

Who is responsible

This portfolio is mine. I, Adrien Maisak, am responsible for the processing of personal data described on this page (the “data controller”). For any question about it, write to me at adrien.maisak@gmail.com.

The portfolio itself

The site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookie, uses no analytics or audience measurement, and loads no script or font from other sites: its fonts and images are served from the site itself. The only exceptions belong to the AI assistant, described below: the home pages ask the assistant’s service whether it is available when they load, and Cloudflare’s anti-robot check loads when you start using the assistant.

Links to other sites (GitHub, LinkedIn, the project demos) take you there only when you follow them, and those sites apply their own policies.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. As the host, GitHub receives the technical data of each connection, such as your IP address, and may record it in its server logs, under GitHub’s own privacy statement.

The AI assistant: what is processed, and why

The assistant answers questions about my background and my projects, and can compare a job offer with my profile. Using it is optional: the rest of the site works without it. It asks for no name, no email address and no account.

What is processed

  • The questions you send, and the answers the assistant gives you.
  • The text of a job offer you paste, or the text read from a PDF you upload, for the time of the analysis only.
  • Technical data: a temporary session identifier, and your IP address, which is used to limit abuse and is stored only in a hashed form (see “How long it is kept”).

Why

  • To answer your questions, and to analyze the job offers you submit.
  • To prevent abuse: an anti-robot check, usage limits per session and per connection, and a daily spending cap.
  • To improve the assistant’s content: the log of questions shows me what visitors want to know, and where the answers fall short.

On what basis

For the conversation, my legitimate interest in presenting my work to the people who visit this portfolio and in protecting the service from abuse. For a job-offer analysis, your own request: the analysis runs only because you ask for it.

How long it is kept

Deletion is done by a cleanup that runs every hour, so each item below can be removed up to about an hour after the time given.

Questions
Kept in a log for 30 days, with the topic, technical measures of the answer (its length, its cost, the time it took) and a hashed IP address. The text of the answers is not part of this log, and for a job-offer analysis the log holds no text at all.
Conversations
Your messages and the answers, which the assistant needs to follow up on a question, are deleted after the two-hour session has expired, and in any case 24 hours after the last message.
Job offers
Neither the file, nor its text, nor the report is saved. A PDF is read in memory to extract its text, then released; only the text, pasted or extracted, is sent to the AI model for the analysis, then dropped.
IP addresses
Never stored in clear. The service keeps only a hash, made with a key that changes every day and is deleted shortly after the day ends: from then on, a hash can no longer be traced back to an address, nor linked to the hashes of other days.
Sessions and counters
Session records and the counters used to limit abuse (identifiers, times and usage counts, with no message text and no IP address in clear) are deleted within about two days. Cost records (usage, cost and a request identifier, with no message text and no IP address) are kept for up to 400 days.
Technical logs
The service’s logs (time, type of request, result, hashed IP address; never the text of your messages) are kept by its host, Fly.io, for the limited time it keeps logs.

Who handles the data on my behalf

OpenRouter, and the AI provider it selects
For each question, OpenRouter receives the question with the latest exchanges of the conversation (as many as the model can take), the portfolio content the assistant reads and a hashed conversation identifier, or, for an analysis, the text of the job offer; it passes them to an AI model run by an inference provider, and returns its answer. The requests ask OpenRouter to use DeepInfra, Fireworks or Parasail first, and, whichever provider it uses, to choose only providers that it lists as not collecting the data they receive and as keeping no prompts or answers after processing (“zero data retention”). These are settings of the request: what each company actually does is governed by its own terms.
Cloudflare
Runs the anti-robot check (Turnstile) when a session opens. Cloudflare’s script runs in your browser and receives technical data such as your IP address and details about your browser; the assistant’s service also sends your IP address to Cloudflare to confirm the check. Cloudflare handles this data under its own privacy policy.
Fly.io
Hosts the assistant’s service, in its Paris region.
Convex
Stores the service’s data: conversations, the question log, session records and counters, in its European region (Ireland).

One more transfer concerns my content, not yours: when I build the assistant’s content on my own computer, the filtered content of every project, including the one the online assistant only knows through its summary, is sent to an AI model provider to write its summary card.

Transfers outside the European Union

Several of these providers are US companies or may process data in the United States. These transfers rely on the safeguards that the providers put in place under their own terms.

What is stored in your browser

The site and its assistant set no cookie. Once you start using the assistant, it keeps up to two entries in your browser’s session storage (sessionStorage), for this site only:

  • askfolio:session:https://askfolio-dayrise.fly.dev: the session token, its expiry time, the session’s usage limits, and the identifier and topic of the current conversation;
  • site:askfolio-log, from your first answered question on: the identifier of the current conversation, your questions and the answers shown; it is cleared when a new conversation starts.

They let the conversation continue when you reload the page or switch between English and French in the same tab. They stay in that tab and are erased when you close it; the token stops working after two hours anyway. A job-offer report is not kept in your browser. Cloudflare’s check runs in its own frame, under Cloudflare’s policy.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask to access your data, to have it erased, or object to its processing. To do so, write to me at adrien.maisak@gmail.com.

The assistant has no accounts and keeps IP addresses only as hashes, so I may not be able to find your questions without details from you: the approximate date and time, and the wording of a question, help. Questions are deleted after 30 days in any case.

You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority: in France, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

Please keep sensitive information out

Do not share sensitive personal information in the chat or in a job offer, about yourself or anyone else: health or political opinions, for example, or the names and contact details of people. Any personal detail you put in a question is kept in the question log. Before analyzing an offer, you can remove the names and contact details it contains.

About the answers

The assistant is an AI: its answers and its job-offer reports may contain mistakes. For anything that matters, check with me directly.
