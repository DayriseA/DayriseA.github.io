Projects

Personal project, live demo

Checkout Surge

10,000 virtual buyers, 500 items, zero overselling.

A simulated flash sale where everyone rushes in at the same moment. My interest was in designing the system behind it.

  • Never sell an item that doesn’t exist, even with duplicate clicks
  • Keep a deliberately slow back office from being flooded
  • Follow each run live on a real-time dashboard
  • Compare design choices side by side, with measured results

Under the hood: an idempotent asynchronous order pipeline, a circuit breaker, a live SSE dashboard and k6 load-test orchestration.

Launch a real load test and watch it live. The demo sleeps when idle, so give it a few seconds to wake up.

Freelance project

AI Social Media Coach

Business owners connect their Facebook and Instagram accounts, describe their business, and get AI coaching based on their own data.

  • Publish, schedule and analyze posts on both platforms
  • Turn each user’s data into a coaching plan with concrete steps
  • Keep users motivated with quests, streaks and rewards
  • Manage several businesses and team members with distinct roles

Under the hood: an async FastAPI backend, an LLM coach with structured outputs, JWT authentication, encryption at rest, Meta- and GDPR-compliant data deletion.
